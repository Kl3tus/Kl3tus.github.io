TextureSnap: 3D Scanner & PBR
com.solidtakegames.texturesnap

1. Information Handling & On-Device Processing

TextureSnap: 3D Scanner & PBR is architected with strict on-device data sovereignty. We do not require account registration, cloud sign-ins, or profile creation.

A. Camera Access & Photogrammetry Capture

TextureSnap requires camera access strictly for:

  • Live camera viewfinder for texture framing and real-time surface scanning.
  • AR feature tracking and multi-angle photogrammetry capture.
  • Generating normal maps, displacement heights, roughness estimators, and 3D mesh geometry.

Zero Cloud Transmission: All 3D meshing (Poisson surface reconstruction, TSDF voxelization, Delaunay triangulation) and texture map computations occur entirely on your device hardware (CPU/GPU/Vulkan). Your camera stream, captured photos, and synthesized 3D models are stored in private app storage or public folders explicitly chosen by you. We never collect, upload, or transmit your raw images or 3D files.

B. Photo Library Access

The app integrates the official Android System Photo Picker. Only image files explicitly selected by you in the system dialog are loaded into the app for texture conversion.

2. Advertising & In-App Purchases

  • Google AdMob: To provide free daily scans, TextureSnap integrates Google AdMob rewarded ads. AdMob may collect coarse device identifiers (AAID), OS version, and diagnostic telemetry in compliance with Google Advertising policies.
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchases for Pro licenses and material packs are processed securely by Google Play Billing. Solid Take Games never sees or stores your payment card credentials.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all scanned textures and exported OBJ/PNG files reside locally on your device, you have complete control over data deletion. Removing the app or clearing its data permanently deletes all local scans.

Orbital Slice: Space Roguelite
com.solidxgames.orbitalslice

1. Information Handling & Gameplay Data

Orbital Slice: Space Roguelite is an arcade action experience that requires no user accounts, registration, or logins. We collect no personal identifiable information (PII).

A. Local Offline Progression

All core gameplay progression data—including unlocked ship hulls, high scores, Talent Lab upgrades, and resonance core balances—is stored strictly offline on your device using standard local app storage.

B. Advertising Telemetry

The game integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to provide free-to-play gameplay and optional rewarded revival mechanics. AdMob may process device-specific information including Google Advertising ID (AAID), device model, coarse IP address, and ad performance telemetry under Google's standard privacy policies.
