Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2026 • Solid Take Games
Summary: Solid Take Games develops privacy-respecting applications. TextureSnap performs all 3D reconstruction and texture synthesis entirely on your device. We do not transmit your photos, scans, or private media to any external servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Solid Take Games ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with our mobile applications and services, including TextureSnap: 3D Scanner & PBR.

2. Information Collection and Processing

A. Camera and Device Storage

TextureSnap requires camera access solely to enable real-time surface scanning, AR photogrammetry capture, and texture map computation. All processing (depth estimation, normal map extraction, meshing, and texture atlas generation) is performed locally on your device's hardware. Your camera stream, captured frames, and generated 3D models are stored in your device's private storage or standard public directories chosen by you. We never collect, upload, or transmit your raw camera images or 3D model files.

B. Photo Library Access

TextureSnap uses the official Android System Photo Picker to let you import existing textures or reference photos. Only the specific image files you select in the system picker are accessible to the app.

3. Third-Party Services

Our applications may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you or display advertisements:

  • Google Play Services: Required for application distribution, licensing, and crash reporting.
  • Google Play Billing: Handles in-app purchases securely through your Google account. We do not receive or store payment card details.
  • Google AdMob: Serves rewarded advertisements to provide bonus scan allocations. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage metrics in compliance with Google's Advertising Policies and Privacy Terms.

4. Data Retention and Security

Because user scan data and textures remain strictly on your local device, you maintain full control over your files. Deleting a scan or uninstalling the app deletes local app data.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.
